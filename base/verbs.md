# Korean basic verbs

## Politeness levels (present tense)

가다 - Dictionary form / infinitive citation form (사전형)
가요 - Informal polite form (해요체), present tense
갑니다 - Formal polite form (합쇼체 or 합니다체), present tense
가 - Plain informal form (해체), casual form

You can get plain form from informal form by simply dropping the final 요, ie 가요 -> 가. Removing 요 works reliably with ordinary endings such as -아요/어요, -지요, -네요, -군요, and -까요, but do not apply it mechanically to -세요 or fixed expressions.

# Past

갔다 - Dictionary form
갔어요 - Informal polite form
갔습니다 - Formal polite form
갔어 - Plain informal form

# Future

갈 + 것이다 -> 갈 거다 - Dictionary form
갈 거예요 - Informal polite form
갈 것입니다 - Formal polite form
갈 거야 - Plain informal form
